A five-minute walkthrough

  1. Company Open the company console in one tab and sign in as admin@finnoto.com.
  2. Customer Open the customer portal in a second tab and sign in as demo@client.com.
  3. Customer Click New issue, choose Urgent fix, pick a category such as Purchase order, add a screenshot, and submit.
  4. Company The bell lights up with an URGENT notification. Open the issue, click Assign to me, type a question, and click Request info.
  5. Customer The ticket changes to Awaiting your reply with your team's question. Reply, and the console gets a new notification.
Customers never see who an issue is assigned to; they see only its status and our replies.
